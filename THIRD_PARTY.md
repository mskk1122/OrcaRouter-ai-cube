# Third-party references

The ST7789 hardware register sequence in `ai_cube/display.py` follows the Waveshare team's `LCD_1inch3.py` and `lcdconfig.py`, retrieved 2026-09-07 from https://files.waveshare.com/upload/8/8d/LCD_Module_RPI_code.zip . The application structure, GPIO mapping changes and face graphics are specific to AI Cube.
